📊 Wyckoff Swing Strategy Rules & Mechanics

Institutional accumulation principles applied to swing delivery trades (5–10 days hold).

🛡️
1. Capital Preservation First
Money is left 100% in safe cash until a stock confirms a high-probability breakout above its trading range resistance. No speculative bottom-fishing.
🌊
2. Wyckoff Accumulation Phases
Algorithms score stocks from 0 to 100 based on Phase A (Selling Climax), Phase B (Volume Absorption), Phase C (Spring & Test), and Phase D (Sign of Strength - SOS).
🎯
3. Profit Target: +20% Delivery
Trades aim for strong 15% to 20% swing expansions within 5–10 trading sessions as institutional markup accelerates past the resistance ceiling.
🛑
4. Strict -5% Stop-Loss
Stop-loss is set tightly below the breakout pivot or support floor (maximum -5%). Capital risk per trade is strictly capped.
🚨
5. Dynamic Trailing Stop (+5%)
Once a position climbs +5%, the trailing stop activates automatically, ratcheting up to protect profits and eliminate downside risk.
⏰
6. 10-Session Time Stop
If a stock moves sideways and fails to reach target within 10 sessions, it is automatically exited to free up capital for fresh high-momentum setups.
